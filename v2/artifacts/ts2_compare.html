Editing models take person + garment and are scored on fidelity first (garment, identity, scene). The realism axis is repaired by a separate auxiliary model — current auxiliary leader seedvr2_x2_noise0, screened separately in v2/runs/aux_compare.html and not part of this page.

Each set starts with the two inputs (person, garment reference) and then every arm's result. duo pairs use a garment reference that is itself a photo of a different person wearing the garment — the model2model case that open models historically collapse on. A red border and [WRONG PERSON] mark identity substitution (the result shows the reference's person).
