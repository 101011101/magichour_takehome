Phase 3 — AC-C, destroy the head

Is destroying the identity enough, without removing the head at all?
All local, all free. Two bases: the original photo with hair kept, and the bald frame. Click to zoom, ← → to step across.
★ Attention Modulation/0 · AC-C destroy/1 · AC-A / AC-B/2 · Crops + PRE/3 · FASHN/4 · AC arms/5 · BG/6 · M
What happens to whatever you approve here: it joins the klein batch already agreed — control (C3.1 as it ships), QX_qwen_p1 (Qwen 2511 extraction), BC_klein (klein-bald → crop) and bald uncropped. Nothing goes to klein until you say which.
  • The idea. Eight iterations went into head removal and each traded precision in one place for damage in another, because removal needs an exact boundary and an error leaves a white notch that gets read as garment. But removal was never the goal — the goal is that the model stops attending to a competing identity, and destruction achieves that without an exact boundary. Over-covering costs a little extra blurred skin: no identity, no garment, and the reference stays a photograph rather than an image with a hole in it
AC-C/O — original photo, hair kept, face destroyed
  • The cheaper branch, and probably the stronger one. No generative call, fully deterministic, no licence question, no hallucination risk, milliseconds to run. If it works the entire bald pipeline is unnecessary
  • It has a measured precedent: C3.2 was “keep the hair, remove the face” and scored 45%, and its recorded failure was “interpreted the white space as cloth” — the hole left by cutting the face out. This is C3.2 with the face destroyed in place: same information removed, no hole created
  • What to judge: is the identity actually gone? A blurred face that still reads as a specific person has not done its job
  • Three blur tiers. Radius scales with the region, not the image, so a small face in a wide frame is destroyed as thoroughly as a large one. Beyond a point repeated passes matter more than radius — n passes at sigma equals one at sigma√n and flattens the region far faster, where simply widening one kernel just grows the halo. light = 1 pass · HEAVY = 3 passes, 2× radius · EXTREME = 5 passes, 3.4× radius, each with a wider feather

AC-C/B — bald frame, head region destroyed
  • Same operations on the AC-B bald crops. Costs a generative call the /O branch does not
  • D4 is the crop fix, run alongside rather than instead, so removal and destruction are compared directly. It uses morphological opening to sever the neck at its own constriction rather than at a guessed line — but that only works when a kernel exists between neck width and head width. It applies on 2 of 11 references and falls back on 9, marked in amber. Predicted in advance that it would not generalise; the prediction was right about the rate and wrong about which — p023 was called the worst case and is one of the two it works on
  • Also visible here: the subject matte includes furniture — a stool and a chair — on several references, and has done in every crop made so far. Found by rendering the masks and looking, not by any measurement
