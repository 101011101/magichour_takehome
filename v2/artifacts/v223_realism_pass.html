The harness was replayed over stored arm outputs, the frame it lands on was taken for each of the 38 sets, and only that frame went through SeedVR2 ×2, noise_scale = 0 — the v2.1 winner. 38 calls, no new generations. This is the first time the v2.1 realism choice has been applied to a v2.2.3 output, which is the "composite never validated end to end" gap.

The change is small and the identity cost is not. Mean absolute pixel change is 2.28/255 — under 1% — and high-frequency energy rises only 12%. Against that, mean identity cosine is 0.933 with a worst case of 0.772, and 7 of 38 frames fall below 0.90. For scale, v2.1 measured this same configuration at 0.943 identity on klein outputs and rejected Z-Image Turbo for dropping to 0.72 — and the worst frame here is 0.77.

Read the identity number with care: AuraFace is comparing a 832×1248 frame against a 2× upscale of itself, so some of the drop is resampling rather than damage. That is exactly why this page exists — the metric cannot settle it and the eye can. Sort order is worst identity first.
