Auxiliary models take one image and may only improve realism — changing content is a failure. Every metric compares each output against its own input (a FASHN v1.5 try-on result), per prd/v2/SCORING_CRITERIA.md. Selection = highest realism among configs clearing the fidelity gate (VLM fidelity ≥ 4.5). Configs rank individually because fidelity preservation depends on strength/noise settings, not just on the model.
