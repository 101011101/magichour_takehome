★ Attention Modulation/0 · AC-C destroy/1 · AC-A / AC-B/2 · Crops + PRE/3 · FASHN/4 · AC arms/5 · BG/6 · M
  • AC0 unrepaired — control
  • AC1 algebra — un-composite B = (I − αF)/(1 − α). Reconstruction, not hallucination; blows up as α→1, so it shrinks the damage to the opaque core rather than removing it
  • AC2 Telea PDE · AC3 xphoto FSR · AC4 xphoto SHIFTMAP patch search — all three zero-weight, so no licence exposure at all
  • feather — orthogonal preprocessing, not an arm: erode the head mask and keep thin strands. The measured damage is an open boundary notch, not an enclosed hole, so not creating it beats repairing it
  • AC5 MI-GAN · AC6 LaMa — learned fillers, CPU TorchScript. Apache/MIT code but Places2-trained weights, whose data terms say non-commercial: these exist to find out whether a learned filler is needed at all
  • AC7.1 klein · AC7.2 Qwen-Image-Edit · AC7.3 Z-Image Turbo — generative repair of the existing crop. 7.2 and 7.3 are mask-native (true inpainting); 7.1 has no mask input so it is generate-then-composite
  • AC8.1 klein · AC8.2 Qwen-Image-Edit — generative crop: the raw photo goes in and the garment comes out, with no cropper involved at all. This is the control on whether the deterministic stack earns its complexity — and the one family that can hallucinate the garment, which a subtractive crop cannot
  • AC9 SeedVR2 restore over the Telea fill. No prompt parameter exists on that endpoint, so it is image-only by necessity
  • For AC8, judge fidelity to the real garment, not just how clean the cutout is. Section 2b showed both instruments reward a plausible garment over the correct one, so a beautiful crop of the wrong clothes is the failure to watch for.
