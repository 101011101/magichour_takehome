# v3.14 — TEST

The matrix, per [SCHEMA.md](../SCHEMA.md). Written before the run; a change to the set is a
new test, recorded as such.

## What is held fixed

Every measurement is on the **production stack**: BiRefNet_lite and the SCHP ATR parser on
CUDA, MediaPipe Selfie Multiclass and Pose on CPU by design, all four pinned by revision or
sha256. The bald pass, where one is needed, is `v3lib.BALD_PROMPT` byte for byte at **seed
46** on the v3.3 canvas — the call of record. No klein setting varies anywhere in this
investigation; klein appears only to produce the bald frames the crop runs on.

**Why the bald frames are regenerated rather than reused:** the crop runs on the bald frame,
not the upload, so a comparison on uploads would not be the production path. The archived
frames live in run zips on a Drive that has been cleared, so the notebook makes them again
from the repo's own photographs. They are deterministic — same photo, same prompt, same seed,
same canvas — so this is a reproduction, not a new draw.

## The set

`v3/colab/v314_set.csv` — **33 garments**, generated by `v3/build/make_v314_set.py`.

| kind | n | source |
|---|---|---|
| worn | 21 | `test_set1` entries whose `photo_style` is `on_model` (13), plus eight fold garments the manifest does not carry as garment rows |
| product | 17 | every `flat_lay` (14) and `ghost_mannequin` (3) entry in `test_set1` |

**Ground truth is the manifest's own `photo_style` field**, not a reading of the picture.

The eight added garments are chosen, not sampled, and for two reasons. Three are dress-heavy
(`p003`, `dualuse_queen_latifah_gown_stage`, `p021`) because that is where candidate B is
expected to struggle. Five are **the collar-guard cases**: `p019` is the garment the guard
exists for — SCHP labels 99.1% of its raised collar `face` — and `p012`, `p028`, `p030` and
`p016` are the ones the guard's tuning was traded against. Candidate A deletes that guard, so
a matrix without them could only flatter it.

## The candidates

| arm | what changes | what it would remove |
|---|---|---|
| **today** | the control — parser head unioned with the selfie `HAIR+FACE` mask, collar guard on | — |
| **A** | `parser_classes(clothes=None)`, no selfie union | Selfie Multiclass, the collar guard, and the inputs both fallbacks need |
| **B** | waist from ATR classes instead of Pose hips | nothing — `parser_classes` calls Pose for the neck line regardless |

Candidate A is measured **only where the parser fires**. Where it does not, today falls back
to the pose ellipse and candidate A has nothing to fall back to; those garments are reported
as *not comparable* rather than scored, because the difference is a missing fallback, not a
reference movement.

## What is measured

**Candidate A**, per garment: mean absolute difference between the two references, the maximum
channel difference, the share of pixels changed by more than 8 levels, and the head mask's
area before and after. The question is not "are they identical" — they will not be — but
**does the difference move the reference**, so the worst movers are shown side by side and
judged by eye.

**Candidate B**, per garment: the parser's waist row and the Pose hip row as fractions of image
height, their absolute difference, and which rule produced the waist (a belt, an upper/lower
boundary, or a refusal). On product shots the question is inverted: the correct answer is to
find nothing, and inventing a waist is the failure.

**Branch inventory and route frequency** are reported for today's pipeline and for each
candidate, because Runbo's concern is how many ways the thing can behave, not how many files
it loads.

## Review protocol

The page (`v3/build/v314_page.py` → `v3/report/v314.html`) leads with the branch inventory and
the route-frequency table, then candidate B's two tables, then candidate A's worst movers as
side-by-side pairs. Nothing is blinded: this is a feasibility comparison against a veto, not
a preference test, and the arms are not interchangeable enough for a blind A/B to mean
anything.

**The bar for adopting either candidate:** no reference moves for the worse, and the branch
count goes down. Failing either, the recorded outcome is *keep what we have*.

**Both candidates were measured locally before this run and both failed that bar** — candidate B
on dresses and on product shots, candidate A on `p019`'s collar. The GPU run's remaining value
is confirmation at the production stack's own numeric footing (the local run used CPU ONNX), and
the branch/route tables, which are what the recommendation rests on.
