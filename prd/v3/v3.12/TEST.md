The matrix, per [SCHEMA.md](../SCHEMA.md). Written before the run and not amended after it.

**What is held fixed.** The shipping path, unchanged: Photoroom's transformer at
`408c457f…` with BFL's text encoder, VAE, tokenizer and scheduler at `e7b7dc27…`; 4 steps;
guidance 0.0; bfloat16; `torch.Generator("cpu")`; call 1 at seed 46 with `BALD_PROMPT` byte for

## 2. The set

`v3/colab/v312_set.csv` — **20 cells** · 10 garments × 2 people × 1 seed (46), generated by
`v3/build/make_v312_set.py`.

**The garments** are the ten product-only photographs of `test_set1` — `g001 g002 g008 g010 g016
g017 g021 g023 g026 g028` — the same ten v3.11 read hips on, so this run's reads are comparable to
the ones on record rather than a fresh sample that would confound the two.

**The people** are the first two person stems of `v311_set.csv` in sorted order, `p001` and
`p002`: both read `full_body` on the framing read, and both are in pairs the v3.10 count marked
clean. Nothing new about the person side enters here — if a cell fails, the garment is the
variable.

## 3. What is recorded

Per garment, joined in `run/meta/v312_flatlay.json`:

- whether Pose reported a hip on the bald frame, its row, and its fraction of the height
- the route each region actually took — cut at the reported hip, or fell back to `full`
- the fraction of the subject the band kept, and the reference's size
- **the hip line drawn on the photograph**, `run/hip/{garment}__orig.jpg` and `__bald.jpg`

The hip is read from the bald frame because that is the image `run_v311.references` reads it from;
the bald pass resizes back to its source, so the row means the same thing in both.

## 5. Review protocol

By eye, on `v3/report/v312.html`, not blind and not counted: the arms are labelled because the
question is *what happens*, not *which is better*. Ten garments at one seed cannot carry a rate,
and none is claimed.

The page is built to make one distinction impossible to miss: a **fallback** is a safe no-op the
system reports, and a **cut at a reported hip** is a silent error it does not. A reader should be
able to say which happened for each garment without reading any JSON.
