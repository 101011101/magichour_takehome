# v3.11 — TEST

The matrix, per [SCHEMA.md](../SCHEMA.md). Written before the run and not changed after it; a
change to the set is a new test. The set of record is `v3/colab/v311_set.csv`, generated by
`v3/build/make_v311_set.py` — the table below renders it.

---

## 1. The arms

Five (arm, region) pairs per cell. The region is the garment type the user chose; the arm is how
the reference was built.

| arm · region | call 1 | reference |
|---|---|---|
| `A · full` | `BALD_PROMPT`, byte for byte | `crop_bc` unchanged — the production reference |
| `A · upper` | the same | mask kept **above** the hip line, then re-boxed |
| `A · lower` | the same | mask kept **below** it |
| `B · upper` | neutralises the **lower** half | the same band, cut from that frame |
| `B · lower` | neutralises the **upper** half | the same band, cut from that frame |

`B · full` does not exist: with nothing unselected there is nothing to neutralise, so B's call 1
would be `BALD_PROMPT` and its reference would be A's. `full` is generated once, under A.

**Call 1 cannot be shared between the arms** — B's prompt differs — so each garment pays three
call-1 passes: one for A, one for `B upper`, one for `B lower`. Within A, one bald frame and one
mask computation serve all three regions.

## 2. The call-1 prompts, verbatim

Arm A, the prompt of record (`v3lib.BALD_PROMPT`):

> Make this person completely bald. Remove all hair from the head and any hair falling over the
> shoulders, chest or back, and show the scalp. Keep the clothing, the body, the pose and the
> background exactly as they are.

Arm B, `REGION=upper` — the user wants the top, so the **lower** half is neutralised
(`run_v311.BALD_NEUTRAL["upper"]`):

> Make this person completely bald. Remove all hair from the head and any hair falling over the
> shoulders, chest or back, and show the scalp. Replace everything they are wearing below the
> waist with plain white trousers, with no pattern, print or logo. Keep what they are wearing
> above the waist, the body, the pose and the background exactly as they are.

Arm B, `REGION=lower` — the **upper** half is neutralised (`BALD_NEUTRAL["lower"]`):

> Make this person completely bald. Remove all hair from the head and any hair falling over the
> shoulders, chest or back, and show the scalp. Replace everything they are wearing above the
> waist with a plain white t-shirt, with no pattern, print or logo. Keep what they are wearing
> below the waist, the body, the pose and the background exactly as they are.

Both keep `BALD_PROMPT`'s first two sentences unchanged — the bald pass still has to happen — add
one sentence naming the replacement, and **rewrite the hold sentence** to hold the half the user
selected. The hold clause of record ("keep the clothing … exactly as they are") cannot stand in
arm B: it contradicts the sentence before it.

**These prompts are new.** No number from v3.8 or v3.10 describes arm B, and none should be
quoted for it.

## 3. The set

`v3/colab/v311_set.csv` — **12 cells**: 6 garments, two people each, seed 46 — giving **60 try-ons**
across the five (arm, region) pairs.

| garment | hip (of frame height) | people |
|---|---|---|
| `g013` | 0.433 | `g004`, `g012` |
| `p006` | 0.594 | `g013`, `p001` |
| `p025` | 0.609 | `p002`, `p012` |
| `p026` | 0.529 | `p003`, `p021` |
| `dualuse_lp_beige_long_coat_menswear` | 0.466 | `p016`, `p026` |
| `dualuse_queen_latifah_gown_stage` | 0.675 | `p012`, `p022` |

**How they were chosen**, all three filters measured rather than eyeballed:

1. **Cells that already work.** Every pair is marked clean in the v3.10 count
   (`v310_count.csv`, arm `NOSCALE`, `fail=0` — 152 clean pairs to draw from). A selector failing
   on a pair that was already broken would say nothing.
2. **Full-body people**, which is Runbo's case: `v3lib.framing` on the person photo, ankle in
   frame ⇒ `full_body`. **22 of the 38** people in clean pairs qualified.
3. **Garments whose hip is readable and mid-frame**: the hip line read off the archived bald
   frame, kept only if it sits between **0.35 and 0.80** of frame height, so both halves are
   substantial. **41 of 56** garments qualified; **18** of those had two clean full-body
   partners; the first six by that ranking were taken.

**The same two people could not cover six garments** — the fold pairs each person with only a
few garments — so the people are chosen per garment from that garment's own clean pairs.

**No product shots.** Runbo's case is a person photograph, and
[EXPERIMENT link 1](EXPERIMENT.md) already measured the flat-lay behaviour: Pose reports a hip on
6 of 10 garments that contain no person.

## 4. Held fixed

- **Call 2**: `ER`, byte for byte (`run_v36.ER`), in every arm.
- **Canvas**: the person's own size under the 1 MP bound, floor 32, never upscaled — the shipped
  rule since v3.10.
- **Sampler**: 4 steps, guidance 0.0, bfloat16, `torch.Generator("cpu")`; call 1 at seed 46.
- **Weights**: Photoroom `transformer_bf16` @ `408c457f` with BFL's text encoder, VAE, scheduler
  and tokenizer @ `e7b7dc27` — the production stack. The runner refuses to start without the swap.
- **Crops on CUDA**, asserted at startup: `onnxruntime-gpu==1.22.0`, both ONNX sessions checked.
- **One seed.** This is a feasibility look; seed variance is already priced elsewhere.

## 5. The fallback rule

A band needs a hip line. The reference falls back to `full` — and records why, per reference, in
`meta/v311_meta.json` — when any of these holds:

| condition | recorded as |
|---|---|
| Pose does not load | `pose unavailable` |
| no pose detected in the frame | `no pose detected` |
| neither hip is confident *and* inside the frame | `no hip in frame` |
| the band would keep < 2% of the subject | `band keeps N% of the subject` |

Never a guessed fraction of the frame. A fallback is a correct outcome, not an error: it means
this photograph cannot be split, and the page marks those references so they are not read as
band failures.

## 7. Review protocol

`v3/build/v311_page.py` → `v3/report/v311.html`. Per garment: the three bald frames with the hip
row drawn on them, then one row per region carrying each arm's reference and its two try-ons.

**Not blind, and no marks are exported.** This is a feasibility look, not a rate: six garments
cannot support one, and hiding which arm is which would only make the page harder to read.

Two questions per try-on, in order:

1. **Did the selected half get swapped?**
2. **Did the unselected half survive untouched** — are the person's own trousers still their own
   trousers when the user asked for a top?

## 8. What would count as working

- **Arm A is enough** if the band cuts at the hip, the reference still holds the whole half it
  claims, and the person's unselected half comes back untouched. Then a selector is a crop change.
- **Arm B is needed** if A's references are ragged at the cut — the seam the stub's old warning
  describes — and B's cleaner, uniform frame fixes them.
- **Neither is enough** if call 2 replaces or invents the unselected half whatever the reference
  looks like. That is the outcome worth knowing early, because it would mean the selector needs
  something the reference cannot carry — a call-2 prompt that names the region, or a mask handed
  to call 2 — and that is a larger piece of work than a crop.
