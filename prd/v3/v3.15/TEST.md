## The matrix — 44 cases

**10 worn-garment requests.** Five garments photographed on a person, each at `upper` and at

| garment | why it is in the set |
|---|---|
| `g004` | a tight top — the ordinary band cut |
| `g024` | a lower-body garment, where `lower` is the meaningful half |

The hip reads that motivate the last two come from archived bald frames made on the BFL
transformer; this run re-makes the bald pass on the production transformer, so they may not fall
back. Either outcome passes, provided it is recorded.

## Pass and fail

A case **fails** if any of these is true:

- it raises;
- a product shot's gate finds a person;
- a product shot runs the bald pass;
- a product shot is cut to a half instead of forced to `full`;
- a worn garment's gate misses the wearer;
- a worn garment is prepared without a bald pass (and not from the cache);
- a worn garment at `upper`/`lower` comes back `full` **without** a recorded fallback reason;
- the applied region is neither the requested one nor `full`;
- a band is applied but `kept_fraction` is not recorded;
- `try_on` makes anything other than exactly one generation;
- the sentence sent to call 2 does not match the applied region (`ER_REGION[region]` for a half,
  the whole-outfit prompt for `full`).

Recorded for every case, expected against actual: gate verdict and head pixels, route, whether the
bald pass ran, requested and applied region, fallback reason, which call-2 sentence was sent,
reference and output sizes, and wall time per stage.

## Budget

| | klein calls |

About 2 minutes of generation on an A100, plus the one-off weight download (~16.5 GB) and model
load. Any GPU with at least 20 GB works — the shipped Install cell enforces that — and a non-A100
only makes the timings incomparable with the record, not the result.

   releases the GPU.
3. Unpack the zip to `v3/runs/v315/a100/` and run `python3 v3/build/v315_page.py`.
