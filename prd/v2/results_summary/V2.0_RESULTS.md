## Run 2 — auxiliary screen (6 configs × 4 FASHN outputs)

Each config scored against **its own input**; gate = VLM fidelity ≥ 4.5.

| config | realism | fidelity | id preserved | hf ratio | gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **seedvr2_x2_noise0** | **4.31** | **5.00** | 0.839 | 2.59 | PASS |
| seedvr2_then_zimage | 4.25 | 4.75 | 0.676 | 2.16 | PASS |
| seedvr2_x2_noise01 | 4.25 | 4.88 | 0.781 | 2.47 | PASS |
| zimage_s025 | 3.88 | 4.63 | 0.490 | 0.80 | PASS |
| zimage_s015 | 3.75 | 4.38 | 0.491 | 0.79 | FAIL |
| zimage_s035 | 3.69 | 3.25 | 0.299 | 0.90 | FAIL |

Findings:
1. **SeedVR2 with noise off is the clear winner** — perfect 5.00 VLM fidelity,
   and it beats fal's default `noise_scale=0.1` on both fidelity (5.00 vs 4.88)
   and identity preservation (0.839 vs 0.781).
2. **Z-Image Turbo drifts identity even at 0.15 strength** (AuraFace 0.49 vs
   SeedVR2's 0.84) despite passing the VLM eye test — direct evidence for
   testing the tile-ControlNet variant, which is the queued self-host test.
3. **No config repairs artifacts.** `artifact_fix` was exactly 3.00 (= no
   change) for all six. A global realism pass does not fix bad hands or
   illogical items — and hands are FASHN's weakest criterion.
4. **`hf_ratio` needs a visual companion.** SeedVR2's 2.59× high-frequency
   energy looked like oversharpening; a 2×-zoom inspection of a plaid print
   showed genuine restoration, and the VLM independently scored
   garment_untouched 5.00. Confound: comparison happens after LANCZOS downscale
   from 2×, which itself crispens.

## Run 3 — `Testset2/` editing models (4 arms × 13 high-res pairs)

51/52 generations succeeded (the one failure is FASHN on ts2_08, finding 3).
`hidream_o1_edit` was added 2026-08-14 via `fal-ai/hidream-o1-image/edit`
($0.01/MP, ~$0.55 for the arm); the other three rows are unchanged.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| klein_4b_edit | **4.41** | **4.03** | **4.08** | 4.46 | 4.69 | 0.903 | 22.9 | 0.811 | 0.00 |
| qwen_2511 (baseline) | 4.28 | 3.95 | 3.77 | 4.31 | 4.77 | 0.784 | 20.6 | 0.726 | 0.00 |
| fashn_v15 | 4.25 | 3.83 | 3.33 | **4.42** | **5.00** | **0.971** | **27.5** | **0.835** | 0.00 |
| hidream_o1_edit | 3.62 | **4.08** | 3.08 | 4.15 | 3.62 | 0.626 | 14.1 | 0.565 | **0.15** |

By pair kind (VLM fidelity):

| kind | fashn_v15 | klein_4b_edit | qwen_2511 | hidream_o1_edit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| garment only (`product`) | 4.22 | **4.67** | 4.50 | 3.78 |
| garment + human (`duo_lookbook`) | 4.33 | **4.42** | 4.33 | 3.58 |
| garment + human (`duo_swap`) | **4.22** | 3.89 | 3.78 | 3.33 |

Findings:
1. **The editing leader is now contested.** FASHN's triage lead did not hold at
   high resolution: klein leads VLM fidelity and realism; FASHN leads the
   deterministic composite, identity (0.971) and background (perfect 5.00 scene,
   27.5 dB). Same shape as V1 — FASHN preserves everything but transfers
   garments conservatively (garment 3.33, lowest of the three); klein transfers
   better but preserves less.
2. **The predicted model2model collapse did not happen.** `wrong_person` = 0.00
   across all 38 outputs — no identity substitution on any duo pair,
   contradicting the VTEdit expectation (Qwen 1.17, klein 1.03 on Model2Model).
   Explicit target-garment prompting plus FASHN's `garment_photo_type="model"`
   appear to be sufficient. Spot-checks confirm: a man's suit + shirt + tie
   transferred onto Emma Watson, a lookbook coat ensemble onto Gal Gadot, both
   with identity and background intact.
3. **FASHN has a hard robustness limit.** One pair failed outright —
   `Failed to detect body pose in model image`. FASHN runs pose detection
   in-pipeline and errors when it fails; klein and qwen have no such
   precondition. A deployment-relevant availability risk, not just a quality
   score.
4. **FASHN wins the hardest kind.** It leads on `duo_swap` (person photo as
   garment source) while klein wins the easy product rows. 3–6 pairs per kind —
   thin.
5. **HiDream-O1-Image is out.** The pixel-native / no-VAE argument that made it a
   dark horse (same reasoning as FASHN) did not survive the set. It **re-renders
   the frame instead of editing it** — scene 3.08 and bg psnr 14.1 against FASHN's
   5.00 / 27.5 dB, with visible reframing and pose change. It **no-ops on
   outerwear**: garment 1/5 on three of the four `duo_lookbook` coats, the input
   outfit left untouched. And it is the **only arm to substitute the reference's
   person** (ts2_08, ts2_11 — wrong_person 0.15, identity cosine 0.05 and 0.10),
   i.e. the model2model collapse the other three arms avoided. Its one win is
   VLM realism (4.08, top of the board), which is an argument for screening it in
   the **auxiliary** bucket, not the editing one.

## Open caveats

1. **Sample size.** 4 pairs (triage), 4 images (aux), 13 pairs (Testset2).
   Everything is directional.
2. **Cross-kind comparison is invalid.** Duo garment scores are inflated
   relative to product because the reference is domain-matched to the output
   (see CONDITIONS.md §3).
3. **Artifacts are unaddressed.** No auxiliary model repairs them; hands remain
   the weakest criterion of the leading editing model. May require a
   region-targeted repair step as a third bucket.
4. **Parity rule outstanding.** All numbers are from fal-hosted endpoints; none
   has been reproduced on downloaded weights.
5. **`hf_ratio` measurement confound** — compare at native resolution against a
   bicubic-upscaled BEFORE in the next round.

## Next

- **Tie-break the editing base:** run both FASHN and klein through the auxiliary
  stage and judge the finished composites. Garment fidelity is the ×2 objective
  (favors klein), but the aux stage repairs realism rather than identity or
  background (favors FASHN as base) — so the base that wins *after* the realism
  pass is the base that ships.
- **Widen the aux screen** — more BEFORE images, plus the zero-drift floor
  (Real-ESRGAN, AuraSR-v2) to make the diffusion refiners' scores interpretable.
- **Self-hosted tests:** Z-Image Base + tile-CN + UltraReal (highest priority),
  then the queued face restorers and the deferred editing arms.
- **Parity run** on downloaded weights for whichever composite wins.
