v3.6 — one word in call 2

The deployed pipeline changes by one verb. BC_klein's call 2 says dress the person in the clothing shown in image 2; ER says replace the clothing in image 1 with the clothing in image 2. Nothing else moves: same reference, same canvas, same seed, same model, same call count. This page is the evidence at a glance; the long read is why nothing more ambitious than this survived.

Read these as they are. The 4.8% is measured — a blind pass over all 600 cells of the iron-man-2 matrix. The ER column is a head-to-head over 150 cells (29 of BC's failures and 121 of its passes), marked with both images side by side. 142 cells were left unmarked as showing no difference worth calling; they are counted as neither a win nor a loss. No cell anywhere on that set was marked BC-better. The headline rate above is not from those marks: it is the same blind sweep over the same 600 cells, one button per cell, no prior verdict in the page — the protocol that produced 4.83%. The two sweeps were not made in the same sitting, so some share of a 1.8-point gap could be a stricter session; the per-cell join (18 repaired against 7 broken) is the claim that does not depend on the two sessions sharing a threshold.

What ships

ER is the arm to deploy — the incumbent pipeline with one verb changed in call 2. Nothing else about the system moves, which is the point: it costs no call, no model and no measurable time.

On top of it, a seed randomiser. A rejected image is redrawn at a fresh seed rather than repaired — and ER's own record is what makes the policy cheap. Both arms are 200 pairs at three seeds, so the sweep says how much of each rate is a seed lottery rather than a broken pair:

BCER
pairs with at least one failure19 / 200 14 / 200
pairs failing at every seed 2 (1.0%) 1 (0.5%)
given a failed cell, another seed passes59% 72%
residual after one retry2.00% 0.83%

ER does not only fail less — its failures are less seed-stable, so a retry is likelier to land. One retry takes it from 3.00% to about 0.83%, and further retries approach the 0.5% floor: the pairs whose reference is wrong, which no seed repairs. Only rejected images are redrawn, so the policy costs about 3% more calls.

The dependency is a rejector, and it is unbuilt. A retry policy needs something that decides an image failed. The v3.6 VLM judge is a first attempt and is not good enough yet: its artifact flag fires on 45% of cells a human passed. Its limb flag is the strongest discriminator found so far (14.8× over base rate) and phasing is usable (2.7×); the artifact bucket is not. That is the next piece of work, and until it exists the retry rates above are a property of the model, not a shipped number.
