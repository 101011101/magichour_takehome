Virtual try-on take-home — final comparison

Output Comparison

What was done

  • Seven hosted models evaluated: Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 (the current baseline), Seedream 5.0 Lite, FLUX Pro VTO v1, FASHN v1.6, FLUX.2 Klein 4B Edit, FLUX.2 Klein try-on LoRA, and Qwen-Image-3 Edit.
  • Staged, budget-gated pipeline: triage → automatic elimination → 12-pair grid → 18-pair held-out benchmark.
  • Three independent judges: deterministic CV harness (pixel-preservation lens), blind GPT-5.5 rubric judge, human review on flagged disagreements.
  • Proposed implementation: composite — Seedream transfers the garment, Qwen-Image-3 applies a realism refine pass.
  • Validated on unseen data: #1 on the blind VLM held-out board (4.22) and above its stage-1 parent on the deterministic composite (0.522 vs 0.507).

Why the composite

  • The rubric judge scores six criteria per output; grouping them into an editing axis (garment, identity, scene) and a realism axis (clean, hands, realism) profiles each model's strengths independently of its overall rank.
  • Seedream: strongest garment transfer, measurable realism deficit. Top garment fidelity on every judge that sees structure (VLM garment 4.00, best garment_sim on the deterministic harness) — but its weakest criteria are the realism ones: hands 3.71, clean 3.96. The output is faithful to the garment but carries residual rendering artifacts (hands, skin, fabric texture).
  • Qwen-Image-3: the inverse profile. At triage the VLM scored it strong on realism and cleanliness (4.0 on both) with weak garment transfer — a good renderer that edits poorly. Useless alone, ideal as a second stage.
  • The composite pairs them: Seedream output piped through Qwen-Image-3 with a strict instruction — fix hands, skin, and fabric textures; change nothing else. Measured on the grid: identity rises 4.33→4.42, garment holds at 4.00.
  • The deterministic harness dissents (a second re-render costs pixel fidelity); decision and dissent are both on record.

Against the production baseline

  • Magic Hour website outputs generated via the official API — unmodified downloads, native 384×576.
  • The production wrapper adds real value: 0.557 deterministic / 3.92 VLM vs raw Qwen 2511's 0.404 / 3.83.
  • Both proposed implementations rank above it on the blind VLM (4.14 / 4.08 vs 3.92); Seedream also edges it deterministically (0.569).
  • Judged weak spots: lowest identity cosine of any arm (one outright person replacement) and garment accuracy 3.25.
