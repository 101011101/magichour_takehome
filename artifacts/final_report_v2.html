Section 01Approach

Goal: a Colab that takes a person image + a garment image and produces the person wearing that garment, demonstrably better than the website's current model (Qwen 2511). Seven hosted candidate models ("arms") were run through a staged, budget-gated evaluation pipeline, judged by three independent systems:

  • Deterministic evaluator harness (authoritative for elimination): garment color-fidelity (HSV histogram, torso crop vs reference), identity (ArcFace face-embedding cosine), pose consistency (MediaPipe landmark displacement), and background preservation (PSNR outside the person mask). Scores combine into a weighted composite (garment ×2) normalized on fixed absolute anchors.
  • VLM judge (gpt-5.5, blind to model names): a six-criterion rubric — garment, identity, scene, clean, hands, realism — schema-validated with self-correcting retries.
  • Human review as the supervising tiebreaker wherever the judges disagreed.

Stages: triage (7 arms × 4 pairs) → automatic elimination (top 50% + baseline) → grid (5 arms × 12 pairs, plus a best-of-2 seed-stability pass on the top 2) → held-out benchmark (18 pairs never seen by any selection decision). Every generation is a reproducible run package; a top-to-bottom re-run reproduces the same elimination and spends nothing on work already done.

Section 02Deliverables

  • The Key — frozen serving configuration. Default implementation: seedream5_lite → qwen_image3 realism-refine cascade; single-model seedream5_lite ships alongside it.
  • MVP eval harness — model-agnostic (one registry entry per new model), stage-agnostic, with deterministic + VLM + human layers; reusable for future model bake-offs.
  • Comparison module — compare_vs_baseline() runs cascade, single seedream, and the Magic Hour baseline on the same inputs, side by side.
  • Evidence — all boards below, plus per-output scores in cv_metrics.csv / vlm_judgments.csv and this report's side-by-side gallery.

Why the cascade: complementary strengths (the checkbox analysis)

The VLM rubric acts as per-model checkboxes. Aggregating them into two axes — editing (garment + identity + scene) and realism (clean + hands + realism) — showed seedream as the strongest transferer while qwen_image3 rendered realistically but edited poorly. The cascade built from that observation was then measured directly on the grid stage: best editing axis (4.33), best garment score of any arm (4.00), identity 4.55 vs plain seedream's 4.33 per the blind VLM. The deterministic pixel-composite did not favor it (re-rendering trades pixel preservation for perceptual quality); the final selection is human review corroborated by the frontier VLM. The baseline was beaten by every judging system on unseen data.
